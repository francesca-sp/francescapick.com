About me

I'm an organizational explorer, ecosystem connector and facilitator with the desire to shape the next generation of organizations and leaders — who are conscious, purpose-centered, and ready to explore a world way beyond business as usual.

What drives me is the belief that how we organize matters more than we think, and aligning our values with how we work, live, and are together is essential for a thriving world.

Much of my practice as an organization developer stems from my practical experience in global self-organized communities — the OuiShare and Enspiral Networks — which I helped grow from their early days starting in 2012, including co-founding OuiShare's flagship event, OuiShare Fest. In 2017 that work led me to co-found Greaterthan, a global collective of practitioners — which has since gone through its own transition and led to the emergence of a new collective, Common Ground.

My interest in systems thinking and organizational development was sparked during my studies at Zeppelin University.

My background

An American raised in Bavaria (Germany), I've always navigated different worlds at once. After being semi-nomadic for many years in Europe, the US and New Zealand, I now live near Barcelona (Spain) with my family. From here, I work globally (mostly online, interspersed with in-person work). Our home is a coliving project, where we welcome friends, family and collaborators, to live, work and play together.

My work

Common Ground

In 2026 I started Common Ground with a group of fellow practioners from its predecesor, Greaterthan Collective. We're a self-organized collective with over a decade of experience with progressive organizational development and commons-based, decentralized, organizing.

I accompany and advise organizations and teams on:

  • shared leadership
  • participatory governance and decision-making
  • self-organizing and postgrowth business
  • value-aligned ways to work with money & resource flow

I often support at thresholds: impact networks ready to mature to their next stage, organizations outgrowing informal ways of working who are looking for an alternative to traditional management structures, and leaders ready to change but unsure of the next step.

Cobudget

I'm steward of Cobudget, an open source tool for collaborative funding and budgeting for groups and organizations.
